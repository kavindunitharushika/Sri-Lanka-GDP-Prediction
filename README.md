# Predicting Sri Lanka's GDP Using Multiple Linear Regression with Macroeconomic Indicators

## Project Overview

This group case study investigates the determinants of Sri Lanka's GDP using selected macroeconomic indicators. The study examines the relationship between GDP and population growth, gross capital formation, domestic credit to the private sector, foreign direct investment net inflows, and trade.

Multiple linear regression is used to analyze the individual and collective effects of these macroeconomic indicators on GDP.

## Research Objectives

1. To identify the impact of selected macroeconomic indicators on Sri Lanka's GDP.
2. To assess the individual effects of each macroeconomic indicator and determine whether their relationships are positive or negative.
3. To construct a Multiple Linear Regression model to predict Sri Lanka's GDP using selected macroeconomic indicators.
4. To evaluate the predictive accuracy and statistical significance of the developed regression model.

## Methodology

- Quantitative Research Approach
- Multiple Linear Regression Analysis
- Least Squares Estimation Method
- Model Fitting and Variable Selection
- Regression Diagnostic Evaluation

**Dependent Variable:** ln GDP

## Macroeconomic Indicators

- Population Growth
- Gross Capital Formation
- Domestic Credit to Private Sector
- Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) Net Inflows
- Trade
